Overview

Tao Tao Ju stands on Dishifu Road in Guangzhou's Xiguan district and is one of the best-known names in Cantonese tea-house dining. It opened in 1880 under the name "Pu Tao Ju" and later became Tao Tao Ju; today the name refers both to the old tea house and to the restaurant brand operated by the Guangzhou Restaurant Group.

Its relationship to the word "teahouse" needs explaining. The core of Tao Tao Ju is not tea drinking on its own but the Cantonese tea-house model of "tea plus dim sum plus Cantonese dishes". In the local phrase "one cup, two pieces", a cup of tea accompanies two dim sum items; tea is part of a meal, not the whole event.

History

The predecessor of Tao Tao Ju, "Pu Tao Ju", opened on Dishifu Road in 1880. In 1891 the Qing reformer Kang Youwei wrote the three characters "Tao Tao Ju", and the name stayed. In 1925 the tea house was expanded to three storeys, making it one of the larger Cantonese tea houses in Xiguan. In 1927, when Lu Xun was in Guangzhou, he also visited the premises.

The late twentieth century brought changes in management. In 2000 the old shop was leased to an outside operator. In September 2018 the Guangzhou Restaurant Group recovered the old shop, and in August 2019 it completed a full acquisition of Tao Tao Ju. At the end of 2021 and the beginning of 2022, the Dishifu Road shop reopened after renovation.

This timeline matters: Tao Tao Ju is not a century-old shop that never closed. Its continuity comes from the name, the address and the Cantonese tea-house craft, not from a simple claim that it traded every day for 140 years.

Tea and space

The space is a classic Cantonese tea house: a large hall, round tables, dim sum stations, trolleys or open kitchens, and a particularly busy morning tea period. Tea choices centre on pu'er, jasmine, oolong and chrysanthemum. Guests sit, order tea first, then call for dim sum in sequence. Har gow, siu mai, char siu bao, chicken feet and rice rolls form the "one cup, two pieces" rhythm alongside the tea.

The menu is not limited to dim sum. As a Cantonese restaurant, Tao Tao Ju also serves roast meats, seafood, stir-fries and banquet dishes; in Guangzhou the line between tea house and restaurant has never been rigid. A visitor who treats it as a tea-only room will miss how it actually eats.

The Dishifu Road building retains the visual character of a Xiguan qilou shopfront and an old-brand sign. The carved "Tao Tao Ju" wooden sign above the entrance is the clearest marker; inside, the circulation has been reorganised for modern restaurant service, combining old-brand symbols with chain-standard operations.

Visiting

Address: 20 Dishifu Road, Liwan District, Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. Status: operating; the old shop and brand branches can be checked through official channels. Checked on 25 November 2026.

Morning tea is usually the busiest period. Opening hours, queueing arrangements and branch locations should be confirmed on the Tao Tao Ju official site or Guangzhou Restaurant Group notices. This is a tea house, not an independent teahouse; plan for a meal with tea rather than a single pot.

Source boundaries

This page uses Tao Tao Ju's official history page and two Guangzhou Restaurant Group notices to support the 1880 Pu Tao Ju opening, the 1891 Kang Youwei inscription, the 1925 expansion, Lu Xun's 1927 visit, the 2000 lease, the 2018 recovery, the 2019 full acquisition, the 2022 reopening and the Dishifu Road address.

Older copy about Kang Youwei holding a long conversation in the shop, a specific comment by Lu Xun, a "number one tea house in Guangzhou" title and the precise origin of a particular dim sum lacks a checkable source and is not repeated. The brand and the old shop are treated according to current public information: Tao Tao Ju is a brand operated by the Guangzhou Restaurant Group, and the Dishifu Road shop is its historical origin.
